Forward-deployed engineering

Status: Last updated: 2026-11-11

Key concept and sub-topics

  • What it is. Engineers embedded with a customer, building working solutions on the customer's real data and systems, instead of shipping a generic product from a distance. The model was popularized by Palantir and is now common at AI companies.
  • Why it fits AI. A model alone rarely solves a business problem. The value comes from wiring it to the customer's data, tools, permissions and workflows, and from evaluating it on their cases.
  • The role is a hybrid. Part software engineer, part solutions architect, part consultant. You write production code, but you also scope the problem, manage stakeholders and decide what is worth building.
  • Typical work. Data integration, retrieval and agent workflows, evaluation sets built from the customer's own examples, guardrails and permissions, deployment, and handoff.
  • Product feedback loop. Patterns that recur across customers get generalized into the core product. That is how a forward-deployed team differs from pure consulting.
  • Key skills. Fast prototyping, data engineering, reading a customer's messy environment, clear communication, and judging when a quick custom build should become a reusable product feature.

AI angle: in agent projects the hard part is rarely the model call. It is access (what data and tools the agent may use), evaluation (what "correct" means for this customer) and trust (guardrails, audit, human review). A forward-deployed engineer spends most of their time there.

Interview questions

How does forward-deployed engineering differ from consulting?The output is shipped, working software, and repeated patterns feed back into a reusable product instead of staying as one-off project work.
A customer wants an agent over their internal data. What do you pin down first?The concrete task and success measure, then which data and tools the agent may access under what permissions, then a small evaluation set drawn from their real cases, before building anything large.
When should a custom customer build become a product feature?When the same need repeats across customers, the solution can be generalized without customer-specific data or logic, and maintaining one shared version costs less than many custom ones.
